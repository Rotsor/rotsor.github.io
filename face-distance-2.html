Hold the device steady. As you move closer, these letters shrink to match; move away and they grow to fill the same share of your view. Your eyes do the focusing — the size just does the rest. Most people can comfortably read printed text down to about twenty-five centimeters before their eyes give up the fight.
This panel zooms rather than reflows: the text's layout never changes, only its scale — so it always breaks onto the same lines, just larger or smaller depending on your distance. Use the slider above to set how large it should read at arm's length (40cm).
